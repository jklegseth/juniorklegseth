What you may want to know:
  • This page generates random headlines based on parameters you can change (you can also leave as default).
  • The words used to generate the headlines were pulled from Google News and BBC Online except per below.
  • Profanity | Profanity | Profanity | Profanity | Profanity (oh, my!). For fun, George Carlin's "Seven Words You Can Never Say On TV" have been added to make things a bit more interesting and to allow the god of randomity to enjoy hi/es/r job.
  • The headlines are temporal, gone as soon as you leave this page or click "Generate Headlines" again.
  • In the Generator Configuration below you can change some settings if you want to.
    • Number of words: Total number of words in the "pool" that the generator draws from. By default, the total number of available words is filled in. Change it as you wish, or not.
    • Number of headlines: how much head you want (how many headlines are generated, with a maximum of 40).
    • Headline length: a range from the fewest words in a headline to the maximum words in a headline. The headlines are then generated between those two numbers, but still randomly. Pretty fucking cool , eh? [fuck or fuck or fuck or fuck]
  • Suggestions:
    • try parameters that might make no sense (it makes perfect sense). For example, try Number of Words = 5, Number of Headlines = 20, From 4 words to 9 words, to get some cool headlines with repetition.
    • limit the Number of words to 100 - 250. Beyond that, sometimes the headlines get out there
    • keep the headline length shorter to make a bit more sense...longer to create complete absurdities
    • ignore all the above
  • Click on any headline to search Google using the words in the headline. This is the hypertextuality. Sometimes the search is useless, sometimes you get a good surprise.
  • Finally, if you get boring results keep clicking Generate Headlines...this usually leads to a set of good headlines.
