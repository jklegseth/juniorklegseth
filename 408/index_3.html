Authors

Starting with Experiment #3, send your story as an attachment to eng408@blinddog.net. All stories received at that address will be posted here for download.

This site began as a repository for an Advanced Narrative Fiction class at California State University - Northridge, where we read, explored, wrote and analyzed experimental fiction. Rather than ditch it now that the class is over, I'll let it linger for a bit, maybe consider adding to it. In the meantime, check out the authors, and don't be afraid—the experiments won't bite!
